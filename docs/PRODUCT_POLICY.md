# CampusWorks product policy

Confirmed by the owner on November 7, 2026.

## Appointments

- A student cannot hold simultaneous TA/RA commitments, including two half appointments and commitments stored under different term IDs.
- Appointment intervals use `[start, end)`: one can finish at the exact instant another begins. The existing one-live-allocation-per-term rule also remains in force.
- An issued offer reserves the candidate until its response deadline. Accepted, cleared, pending-start, active and suspended appointments reserve their corresponding dates until resolved or ended.
- Instructors and administrators use the existing appointment suspension/end and manual course-permission controls. There is no student resignation request workflow.
- Appointment-derived access remains scoped to its TA course; RA appointments have no operational workspace. Ending that appointment preserves separately granted manual roles.

## Administration and documents

- Keep the existing four roles and course/site administration scopes. Do not add department administration or an independent second-approver workflow.
- Do not add retention/purge policies, PDF extraction, antivirus services or HR/payroll integrations in this scope.
- Existing private attachment controls, quotas and restricted post-offer information remain in place. These controls do not imply external HR or antivirus validation.

## AI assistance

- Candidate-list, qualification and availability changes automatically invalidate the old comparison and schedule an updated comparison for its established reviewer and scope.
- Automatic comparisons remain advisory, use current permissions and evidence, and obey the existing daily and concurrent request budgets.
- Duplicate changes and overlapping worker executions must not produce duplicate calls for the same current evidence.
- Missing provider configuration means no model call. The owner supplied an OpenAI Key through a masked local form; provider verification is recorded separately.
- Automatic comparison does not select, reject, approve clearance, change roles or alter budgets.

## Presentation and verification

Preserve the Duke blue visual system, the Duke Computer Science PNG and the established four-role workflows. Check current logic and presentation together through local multi-instance, concurrency, browser, accessibility and responsive-layout tests. Keep the interactive website and demonstration windows closed as requested. Run automated browser checks in isolated test processes.

## Recruitment entry and semester

- Entry links belong on the course-list home page, not inside individual course queue/manager pages.
- Hide unavailable controls and redirect unauthorized management routes home; enforce the same authority on the server.
- Only current instructors and administrators manage positions and hiring. TA/student identities may manage their own applications and offers.
- Administrators choose one current semester and application window. Ordinary users cannot override the semester or deadline.
- Closing applications preserves faculty review and already-issued offers. Disabling recruitment stops review/selection; changing semesters blocks new actions in the former cycle without cancelling existing appointments.
- TA and RA share application, offer and appointment controls. No research project workspace is included.
- Keep optional explanatory copy out of the application interface.
