# CampusWorks roadshow

Open [the eight-slide PowerPoint](CampusWorks-Roadshow.pptx) for an approximately five-minute presentation. Native text, the lifecycle diagram and the stakeholder table remain editable; screenshots retain their original product pixels. The deck embeds its English speaker script and source references. [The PDF](CampusWorks-Roadshow.pdf) provides a portable reading copy, and [the presenter notes](PRESENTER_NOTES.md) give the timed script.

The story covers fragmented recruitment, the applicant-to-TA lifecycle, crowded office hours, real recorded AI examples, department buyers, stakeholder benefits and a proposed pilot. All product records shown are synthetic. The initial buyer, subscription and expansion paths are proposals. Institutional SSO and bounded wait estimates are future targets.
